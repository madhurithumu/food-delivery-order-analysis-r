# Conclusion

Analysis of 1,992 cleaned food delivery orders (Hyderabad, 2025) shows that Biryani is the most ordered category (26.4% of orders), while Pizza has the highest average order value (Rs 417). Demand is concentrated in Gachibowli, Madhapur and Hitech City (49.4% of orders), peaks at lunch (12:00) and dinner (19:00), and is higher on weekends. Monthly orders rose from 117 in January to 213 in December, and UPI is the preferred payment method (52.5%).

Delivery performance affects customers: peak-hour deliveries average 35.90 minutes against 32.44 off-peak (Welch t-test, p < 2.2e-16), Secunderabad and Dilsukhnagar are slowest (40.7 and 42.4 min), and longer delivery times go with lower ratings (r = -0.39). Better-rated restaurants also receive more orders (r = 0.835).

Recommendations: add rider capacity during 12:00-14:00 and 18:00-21:00, review logistics in the two slowest areas, and promote high-value categories such as Pizza and Biryani.

Limitations: median imputation reduces variance, 98 orders have no rating, correlations do not prove causation, and the data covers one year only.
